Lomond Robotics provides custom technical solutions for companies and labs that need AI-harness implementation, laboratory automation, robotics implementation, or bespoke lab solutions, sized to the job, not to a vendor's standard contract.

## AI-harness implementation

Practical scaffolding around AI models for lab and engineering use: tool use, data interpretation, decision support, and evaluation, integrated with existing instruments and processes. The focus is reliability and auditability, not a chatbot bolted on top of a workflow that needs to be trusted.

## Laboratory automation

Design and implementation of systems that reduce repetitive manual work, improve consistency, or connect instruments and processes together: liquid handling, sample processing, instrument control, and workflow automation, scoped to your throughput rather than a one-size-fits-all platform.

## Robotics implementation

Robotic arms, end-effectors, motion control, sensors, actuators, controllers, and embedded systems, integrated into lab or bench workflows. This includes selecting components, wiring and control, data capture, commissioning, and troubleshooting.

## Bespoke lab solutions

Full turnkey builds: measurement devices, one-off fixtures, proof-of-concept systems, and complete instrument supply, sourced, configured, integrated, and handed over as one working system. 3D printing can be part of this when it is the right tool, but it is only one part of the process.

## Data processing and analysis

Custom pipelines for capturing, cleaning, storing, and visualising measurement data from instruments, sensors, or lab equipment.

## On-demand engineering support

If the problem is unusual, uncertain, or not yet fully specified, Lomond Robotics can help turn it into a clear technical plan and then build the right level of solution.
