Lomond Robotics helps labs and engineering teams solve practical problems in four areas: AI-harness implementation, laboratory automation, robotics implementation, and bespoke lab solutions.

The work is not limited to 3D printing. Prototyping can be part of the process, but the main value is understanding the requirement, choosing the right technical approach, and turning it into something usable, often at a scale that a large automation vendor's standard platform doesn't fit.

Typical work includes custom automation, robotics and hardware integrations, measurement devices, engineering prototypes, and practical AI-harness implementation inside lab and company workflows.

Projects can start as a short consultation, a feasibility check, a prototype, or a full custom solution.
